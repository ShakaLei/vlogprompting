V.LOG.PROMPT.ING — talk to camera, not to keyboard

On August 12, 2026, Shaka Lei Kaumaka was vlogging with his AI ʻohana — not typing, just talking to the camera — when it hit him: a vlog is a prompt. You speak, the transcript becomes the instruction, and a fleet of agents runs the play before the video is even posted. So he said it out loud, mid-video:

"By the time we post this video, we will own vlogprompting.com."
— and this door was live before the video ended. This page is that promise, kept.

Vlog prompting is the loop: you vlog → the words get transcribed → the transcript becomes a prompt → agents act. No keyboard, no prompt engineering course, no blank text box. Just you, talking, and an ʻohana of agents doing the rest:

1 · VLOGyou talk to the camera — the idea, the wish, the plan, raw and real
2 · TRANSCRIBEevery word captured — the LISTEN station of The Karaoke Protocol does this: word-by-word whisper transcription
3 · PROMPTthe transcript becomes the instruction — no translation layer, your voice IS the prompt
4 · ACTthe agents run it — doors get bought, pages get built, repos ship, while you're still recording

This isn't a demo page. This page is the artifact: the door was named on camera, bought mid-conversation, and wired live by agents — the loop, end to end, on its very first run. If you're reading this, vlog prompting works.

The keyboard had a good run. The camera is next. Every kid with a phone and a dream can now prompt a fleet — in their own words, their own voice, their own language.
